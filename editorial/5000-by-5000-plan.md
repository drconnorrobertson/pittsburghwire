# Pittsburgh Wire: 5,000 articles and 5,000 business profiles

Authorized October 25, 2026. Targets are separate: 5,000 distinct substantive editorial articles and 5,000 verified business profiles. Archive indexes, category/service/community hubs, aliases, refreshes and withdrawn pages do not count as new articles or businesses. Starting inventory: 489 archive articles and 2,801 verified profiles. This inventory is not an assertion that every legacy page meets the long-form standard.

## Publication standard

New editorial pieces: normally 600–1,000 useful words, longer when evidence warrants it. Distinguish reporting from evergreen guides; never present an old announcement as new news. At least two directly relevant authoritative sources or a primary source with sufficient firsthand detail. Attribute forecasts, promotional claims and dated facts. No fabricated interviews, visits, awards, rankings, owners, hours, prices or business history.

New featured business profiles: normally 600–900 words of individually researched body copy, supported by multiple source pages. Shared navigation, boilerplate and category guidance do not satisfy that target. Short verified listings may remain useful, but are tracked separately from long-form features. Do not add filler to hit a word count. If reliable material is insufficient, keep the candidate in research or expand the existing page when evidence becomes available.

## Link structure

Each profile links to its official site, evidence, directory/category and relevant reporting. Each article links to the businesses it actually discusses and the appropriate directory or location collection. Prefer an existing canonical guide over new neighborhood/keyword variants serving the same reader need. Add reciprocal profile-to-story links through the existing builder. Use descriptive anchors; do not link every repeated mention. Preserve established URLs and historical dates when refreshing.

## Milestones

1. Inventory and audit legacy depth, duplicate intents, missing evidence, broken links and stale statuses.
2. Reach 3,000 verified business profiles and 600 editorial articles, with separately reported long-form counts.
3. Reach 3,500 profiles / 1,000 articles, then 4,000 / 2,000, 4,500 / 3,500 and 5,000 / 5,000.

These are checkpoints, not deadlines or indexing promises. Reassess sources and audience usefulness at each checkpoint. No automatic creation of thousands of pages from business names or search phrases.

## Research lanes

Manufacturing/robotics and supplier services; independent retail and local makers; food and hospitality; property and professional services; cultural institutions and learning; Pittsburgh neighborhoods and surrounding boroughs. Source regionally from official operators, municipal/county releases, institutions and established chambers. Verify current local identity and deduplicate existing catalog/source records before writing.

## Release workflow

Research a coherent small cluster; check existing coverage; write unique articles and profiles; add evidence and related links; rebuild; run quality_check.py, check_business_catalog.py and scripts/content-inventory.py. Compare existing catalog warnings with the baseline. Do not count unparsed bodies as zero-length articles. Review generated changes, publish, verify live content and notify IndexNow for changed URLs only. Use direct signed-in Search Console; never use GSC Wizard. Submission acceptance is not indexing. Record the release and remaining work in the inventory and editorial log.
