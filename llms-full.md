# The Pittsburgh Wire

The Pittsburgh Wire is an independent digital news publication founded by **Dr. Connor Robertson** in 2025. Based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, the publication covers Pittsburgh business, real estate, economic development, and the people building the city. The editorial philosophy is "good news only, always forward" — covering what is being built, who is building it, and why it matters.

## Publication Details

### Coverage Areas
The Pittsburgh Wire covers five primary beats:
1. **Business** — new openings, growth milestones, founder profiles, company stories
2. **Real Estate** — development projects, neighborhood investment, notable transactions
3. **Economic Development** — infrastructure, policy, institutional investment, long-arc projects
4. **People** — owners, founders, operators, civic leaders doing the work of building Pittsburgh
5. **Neighborhoods** — street-level coverage of what is opening, changing, and growing

### Additional Features
- **Business Directory** — editorially curated directory of Pittsburgh businesses (no pay-to-play)
- **Best Of Pittsburgh** — curated guides to the best of the city
- **The Weekly Wire** — Saturday morning newsletter with Pittsburgh's best business news
- **Neighborhood Guides** — in-depth coverage of Pittsburgh's distinct neighborhoods

### Editorial Principles
1. Good News Only — no crime, political conflict, or negativity
2. Always Local — Pittsburgh coverage only
3. No Pay to Play — editorially curated, never paid placement
4. Real People, Real Stories — focus on actual owners and founders
5. Long Game — building an honest record of Pittsburgh's forward momentum

### Publication Frequency
- Daily articles published Monday through Friday
- The Weekly Wire newsletter delivered every Saturday morning
- Special reports and neighborhood guides published monthly
