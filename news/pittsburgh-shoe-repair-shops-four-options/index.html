Pittsburgh Shoe Repair: Four Shops to Compare

Compare four Pittsburgh-area shoe repair businesses by their published services, locations and intake instructions, with Squirrel Hill Shoe Repair as our lead editorial selection for its broad menu and walk-in assessment.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 12, 2026 3 min read

Pittsburgh shoe repair businesses offer several ways to keep familiar footwear in use, from a walk-in assessment in Squirrel Hill to on-site Birkenstock work in Bloomfield. The useful comparison is what each shop publishes about the repair process, the footwear it handles and how customers start an enquiry.

Our lead editorial selection is Squirrel Hill Shoe Repair for its combination of a broad repair menu and clear walk-in instructions. These four selections compare documented services and practical access, rather than customer-review scores or an independent quality award. Information was checked against the businesses’ websites on October 12, 2026; confirm hours and the proposed work before travelling.

1. Squirrel Hill Shoe Repair: a clear starting point

Squirrel Hill Shoe Repair, at 2205 Murray Avenue, Suite 2, publishes heel replacement, resoling, boot rebuilding, leather restoration, zipper and stitching repair, and stretching. Its separate service categories cover designer shoes, Birkenstocks and sneaker cleaning, making it a useful first enquiry for households comparing several kinds of footwear.

The shop explicitly says no appointment is needed. Listed hours are Tuesday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.; Monday and Sunday are closed. Call 412-521-3355 for questions about a particular pair. Our sourced business profile gathers its contact and service details. The reason for the lead selection is this published breadth and straightforward intake process; an assessment still determines what can be repaired.

2. Best-Made Shoes: Birkenstock repairs in Bloomfield

Best-Made Shoes lists its address as 5143 Liberty Avenue and hours as Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Its website specifically discusses repairs to Birkenstock soles, cork, leather straps and footbeds, and says repairs are performed on-site.

That makes it a relevant alternative for someone whose main question concerns a worn sandal component. Call 412-621-9363 and describe which part needs attention. Ask whether the proposed job replaces an individual component or involves more extensive rebuilding, and request the total estimate before agreeing to work.

3. Northway Shoes and Repair: repair and fitting in Ross

Northway Shoes and Repair operates at 6200 Babcock Boulevard in Ross Township. The business identifies Tony DeMarco as its cobbler and says it can resole almost all shoes and boots. It also describes measuring and fitting customers for footwear, which gives shoppers a repair enquiry and a retail fitting option at the same business.

The published schedule is Monday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Sunday, noon to 5 p.m. Call 412-367-7977 to confirm repair intake and collection arrangements. Ask specifically whether your shoe construction is suitable for the requested sole replacement.

4. Al’s Shoe Repair: on-site work in Bethel Park

Al’s Shoe Repair, at 1048 Logan Road, says men’s and women’s shoe and boot repairs are completed on-site. It advertises free estimates, Allen Edmonds repairs, UGG cleaning and waterproofing, and work on items including purse straps and zippers.

The business asks customers to call 412-417-7589 to schedule an appointment and describes flexible drop-off and pickup arrangements. Confirm those arrangements directly instead of assuming a fixed daily counter schedule.

Before leaving footwear with any shop, agree on the repair, material choice, expected collection date and total cost. Pittsburgh’s neighborhood repair businesses offer different routes into the same decision: whether a specific pair can serve you longer with appropriate work.
