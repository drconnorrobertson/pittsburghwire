1930 Cigar Bar Lists Steelers Watch Party for Oct. 18

The Strip District lounge has a prepaid game-day experience listed for Pittsburgh’s road matchup with Tampa Bay, with a noon start, wings and four screens at 2350 Railroad Street.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 12, 2026 3 min read

1930 Cigar Bar has a Steelers watch-party reservation listed for Sunday, October 18, when Pittsburgh visits Tampa Bay. The prepaid experience is scheduled to begin at noon at 2350 Railroad Street in the Strip District, an hour before the 1 p.m. kickoff. OpenTable lists the event at $30 per person and caps reservations at 20 guests, making it a useful option for fans who want a planned place to watch the away game.

The date and kickoff are confirmed on the Steelers’ official 2026 schedule. The OpenTable event listing identifies the watch party as a prepaid experience for October 18 only. Event details can change, so check the booking page again before traveling or buying a ticket.

What the ticket listing includes

OpenTable says the ticket includes all-you-can-eat wings, game-day snacks, one complimentary cocktail and access to four large-screen TVs. The page also describes couches and comfortable seating. It does not specify a separate menu for the event or identify additional food and drink costs, so guests should read the current checkout terms and ask the venue about any needs that are not covered by the listing. The published group limit is one to 20 people.

The noon start is earlier than the cigar bar’s standard posted hours. The company’s official website lists the Strip District location as open daily from 4 p.m. to midnight and gives its address as 2350 Railroad Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15222, with phone number (412) 329-6197. Treat the earlier time as an event-specific schedule, not a change to the lounge’s regular hours. Use the reservation listing for event entry details.

A cigar lounge with a full food and drink offer

1930 Cigar Bar is one of three McDonnell-family concepts at 2350 Railroad Street, alongside Atria’s and Juniper Grill. The company’s grand-opening announcement says all three marked their opening on April 1, 2026. The cigar bar’s concept pairs premium cigars with cocktails, bourbons and fine wines; its food menu includes shareable plates, entrees and dessert inspired by 1930 by Atria’s. The VisitPITTSBURGH business listing provides that overview, while the 1930 brand’s official menu links point visitors to current cigar and spirits lists.

That combination gives the watch-party listing context: this is a dedicated Strip District lounge with a dining offer, rather than a stadium event. Visitors can use the company’s posted hours for a regular visit and reserve through the event page for the October 18 game. Because the reservation is prepaid and time-specific, confirm the ticket’s current availability and terms before setting plans.

For group planning, the distinction between the scheduled experience and everyday hours matters. The booking page marks this as an October 18-only, prepaid event for one to 20 guests; the operator has separately said the cigar bar can host private events. Check the event’s current availability and full checkout terms before purchasing, and use the venue’s listed phone number for questions about arrangements not covered online. The watch-party details are specific to this date and should not be assumed for other Steelers games.

For a Sunday game plan, the key details are straightforward: book the listed noon experience, allow time to reach Railroad Street, and check the reservation page for updates. The Pittsburgh Wire reviewed the public company, venue and event pages on October 12, 2026; no interview was conducted for this report.
