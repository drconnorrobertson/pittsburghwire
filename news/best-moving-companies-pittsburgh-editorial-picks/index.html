Best Moving Companies in Pittsburgh: 3 Editorial Picks

Compare South Hills Movers, TWO MEN AND A TRUCK and Bellhop for Pittsburgh-area moves. Our editorial shortlist distinguishes full-service, packing and labor-only options, with official enquiry links and planning questions.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 10, 2026 3 min read

The best moving company for a Pittsburgh relocation depends on the route, the belongings and how much help you need. A full household move and labor to load a rented truck are different purchases. These three editorial picks publish distinct service options. South Hills Movers leads our shortlist for its regional base and range of residential and business services. This is a publisher selection, not a tested ranking of reliability or price.

1. South Hills Movers — our lead regional comparison

South Hills Movers lists its office at 3132 Industrial Boulevard, Bethel Park, PA 15102, and telephone 412-835-8600. Its website describes local and long-distance moves, employee relocation and office moving. The business identifies itself as an independent carrier rather than an affiliate of another van line.

Its local service description names Allegheny, Butler, Beaver, Washington and Westmoreland counties. That regional coverage and the separate office-moving offering are our reasons for featuring it first. Supply both addresses and the type of move when requesting a proposal; an office relocation needs a different inventory from a household move.

The online quote form asks for an estimated move date, origin, destination and service type. Prepare those details, then explain any storage interval or restricted delivery window. A submitted enquiry does not reserve a crew, establish a delivery date or confirm the final price.

2. TWO MEN AND A TRUCK — moving, packing and in-home work

The Pittsburgh TWO MEN AND A TRUCK page describes local moving, long-distance moving, packing services, furniture rearranging and boxes and supplies. Its published service areas include Pittsburgh, Mt. Lebanon, Bethel Park, Wexford and Squirrel Hill.

The separate furniture-rearranging option is useful to distinguish from a move between addresses. If you need help shifting items within a house or preparing a property, describe that job explicitly. Ask what crew, equipment and minimum booking apply instead of assuming the terms match a standard household move.

The page also describes junk removal through a sister company. Confirm which company is providing each service and whether removal and transportation require separate arrangements. If you are packing yourself, ask for the current list of excluded items and how items should be prepared before the crew arrives.

3. Bellhop — compare labor-only and full-service choices

Bellhop's Pittsburgh page advertises local, long-distance and labor-only moving, with packing among its customizable options. It provides online quotation and telephone enquiries at 412-515-3979.

The labor-only distinction matters if you already have a truck or container. Ask whether the proposed booking includes transportation, loading, unloading or some combination, and identify who supplies equipment. Two quotes described simply as moving help can cover very different responsibilities.

Describe stairs, elevators, loading access and any particularly large items before accepting the proposal. Confirm who will handle your booking and how changes to the inventory or date affect the agreement. The service menu alone does not settle the terms of a particular move.

Compare the complete written scope

Give each mover the same inventory and access details. Ask for the total estimate, applicable minimums, travel charges, packing materials, deposit and cancellation terms. Clarify the arrangements for damage reporting and any declared-value protection offered in the agreement. Keep a copy of the confirmed scope and arrival window. The official sources linked here establish advertised services; confirm availability and the arrangements for your specific route directly.
