McCandless Restaurant Week: 5 Specials to Try

The first-ever McCandless Restaurant Week runs Oct. 12–16 with 18 participating businesses. These five picks include a seasonal latte, Vietnamese takeout and practical meal bundles.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 12, 2026 3 min read

McCandless’ first Restaurant Week runs Monday, Oct. 12, through Friday, Oct. 16, with 18 participating businesses, according to the Town of McCandless event page. The town calls each participating offer a TOM Special. This guide highlights five options chosen for variety and clear, practical details—not as a ranking. Check the linked town listing for each offer’s current terms before ordering.

1. Lucky Bug Coffee: a seasonal apple latte

The Lucky Bug Coffee ordering page lists the shop at 820 West Ingomar Road. Its Restaurant Week feature is a salted caramel apple latte made with house-made apple syrup, available hot or iced. The town lists it at $5.75 small or $7 large. It is an easy coffee stop to pair with errands in the North Hills; use the town’s special listing for the offer details.

2. Commonplace Coffee: coffee and breakfast at Northland

At Commonplace Coffee inside Northland Public Library, the $15 Restaurant Week pairing combines an exclusive Library Ghost Latte with a Spooky Breakfast Burrito. The cafe is at 300 Cumberland Road, and Commonplace says seating is available on the library’s first and second floors. The town’s specials page invites customers to check out a book or board game with the order, making this a sit-down option as well as a quick breakfast.

3. Little Viet Kitchen: a takeout rice plate

Little Viet Kitchen operates as takeout at 195 Blazier Drive and lists Tuesday-through-Sunday hours from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Its $20 TOM Special pairs a roasted chicken rice plate—a new menu item for the event—with passionfruit lemonade, according to the town’s official offer page. Because the restaurant is closed Mondays, this one is best planned for Tuesday through Friday during the event rather than assumed available on opening day.

4. OTB at the Boathouse: a North Park lunch

At OTB North Park, the town lists a new Chicken Parmesan Sandwich with a side salad for $14. The restaurant’s North Park location is 10301 Pearce Mill Road in Allison Park. The boathouse setting makes it a useful choice for someone combining a meal with time in the park; the restaurant’s site posts location, menu and operating information, while the TOM listing confirms the event special.

5. Hello Bistro: a bundled salad meal

Hello Bistro at McCandless Crossing is at 701 Providence Boulevard and lists daily hours of 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Its $14.99 Roasted Ranch Salad Meal includes a side and drink, according to McCandless’ specials page. This is the clearest option in the group for diners seeking a stated meal bundle instead of a single new item. The restaurant’s site also supports pickup and delivery ordering at this location.

The town’s Restaurant Week page lists all 18 participants and says diners can ask a participating business for a McCandless Restaurant Week Passport. The passport carries additional offers that may be redeemed through Dec. 1, 2026; ask for one during a visit and check its individual terms. Since the event is limited to Oct. 12–16, hours, dine-in restrictions and availability vary by business. Confirm details directly before making a special trip, especially for offers the town labels dine-in only.
