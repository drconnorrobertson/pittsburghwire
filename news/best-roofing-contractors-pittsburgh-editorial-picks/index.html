Best Roofers in Pittsburgh: 3 Editorial Picks

Compare Resnick Roofing, Buccos Roofing and McClellands using published services, contact details and estimate processes, with practical questions to ask before choosing a contractor for repair or replacement.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 10, 2026 3 min read

The best roofer for a Pittsburgh property depends on the work: investigating one leak, replacing a whole roof, or coordinating roofing with gutters and siding. These three editorial picks offer different starting points for that conversation, with published services readers can compare before requesting a site visit.

Resnick Roofing & Contracting leads our shortlist for its combination of roof repair, replacement and exterior services. This is an editorial selection based on official service information checked October 10, 2026, not an independent award, customer-review ranking or account of work we personally commissioned. Numbering does not establish that one contractor will suit every building.

1. Resnick Roofing & Contracting: compare a broader exterior project

Resnick’s official website lists residential and commercial roofing, repairs, siding and gutters. That makes it our lead enquiry for an owner who wants to discuss the roof and connected exterior work together, rather than collect separate descriptions of each job.

The North Hills location is 5320 William Flynn Highway in Gibsonia, PA 15044. The company also publishes Canonsburg and Murrysville locations and a contact number of 412-307-9809. Give the team the property address when asking which location will handle the enquiry.

One useful qualification appears on the website: its free-quote offer applies to full replacements only. Ask about charges for a repair assessment before scheduling. Our updated Resnick Roofing business profile brings the current contact information and this guide together.

2. Buccos Roofing: start with the existing roof material

Buccos Roofing’s Pittsburgh repair page lists asphalt, slate, metal and tile repairs, along with leak, flashing, vent and storm-damage work. It publishes 724-554-6224 and an online enquiry form. This is a useful alternative to consider when explaining the existing material is central to the job.

The published repair process includes inspection and diagnosis, an itemized quote, then repair and cleanup. Ask whether the inspection offer applies to your particular property and request the proposed repair scope in writing. A published answering service is not confirmation that a crew can reach a specific address immediately; establish the appointment and any temporary protection arrangements directly.

3. McClellands Contracting and Roofing: discuss related remodeling

McClellands Contracting and Roofing lists residential roof inspection, repair, replacement and installation, plus commercial roofing services. Its wider offerings include siding, gutters and interior remodeling, giving owners another option when the roof is part of a larger property project.

The company publishes 412-353-5660 and describes an estimation step involving a home visit, inspection and detailed quote. Its website names Bridgeville, Sewickley and Ross Township among the communities it serves. Confirm your exact address and the requested work rather than assuming every listed service is available everywhere. For a combined project, ask for separate roofing and remodeling line items so the proposal remains comparable.

Compare the proposed work before choosing

Send each contractor the same description, including the roof material if known, visible damage, previous repairs and photographs taken safely from ground level. Ask the written proposal to identify materials, flashing work, disposal, cleanup and how unexpected decking repairs would be priced. Request the warranty terms for the specific installation being proposed.

Keep repair and replacement options separate when both are offered. A lower total may reflect a smaller scope rather than a better value. Confirm inspection charges, scheduling and the person responsible for follow-up before agreeing to work; this shortlist is a starting point for those decisions.
