Two Pittsburgh-Area Yarn Shops: Project Supplies, Classes and Stitch Groups

Compare Oakmont and McCandless shop information, class-material rules and social crafting; confirm current schedules before a visit.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

A Pittsburgh-area yarn-shop visit can serve several different purposes: buying materials for a pattern, joining other makers for social crafting or registering for instruction. Comparing those arrangements before traveling helps avoid assuming that a drop-in group includes a lesson or that a class fee includes all supplies.

This October 13, 2026 shortlist compares Oakmont Yarn Collaborative and Yarn Bits and Bobs using official shop information. Oakmont leads for its clearly published class-material rules and separate open-stitch schedule. Yarn Bits and Bobs offers a North Hills alternative with supplies, classes and social crafting. This is an editorial comparison of practical information, not an independent ranking of yarn quality or instruction.

1. Oakmont Yarn Collaborative: classes with explicit supply requirements

The location page lists 622 Allegheny River Boulevard, Oakmont. Published hours are Tuesday through Saturday, 11 a.m.–5 p.m., extended to 8 p.m. Thursday; Sunday and Monday are closed. Confirm special closures before a trip. The contact number is 412-794-8332.

The class policies require full payment to reserve a place and say classes are for adults eighteen and older unless specified otherwise. Materials are additional unless a listing expressly includes them, and students must use yarn purchased at the shop unless an exception is stated.

The shop offers ten percent off in-store class-supply purchases. Compare the registration charge with the yarn and tools needed for the actual project, rather than treating the class price as the complete cost.

Class fees are non-refundable. Cancellations at least three days before the first class qualify for store credit; shorter notice does not. Missed classes do not receive make-ups or credits. If the shop cancels, it offers a refund or rescheduling. Check those terms and the chosen class listing before registering. Our Oakmont profile links the official routes.

For a different format, the events page lists open stitch groups Tuesday 1–3 p.m. and Thursday 6–8 p.m., welcoming crafts and crafters without registration or a long-term commitment. Bring the project you are working on. That social format should not be assumed to provide the same instruction as a paid class.

2. Yarn Bits and Bobs: North Hills supplies and social crafting

The official website identifies an independent McCandless shop at 8105 Perry Highway, Pittsburgh, PA 15237. It sells yarn, knitting needles, crochet hooks, notions and project supplies in person and online, and advertises knitting and crochet classes.

The homepage lists open crafting Wednesday 6–8 p.m., Friday 2–4 p.m. and Sunday 2–4 p.m. It also has an October 1 holiday-hours banner alongside a different regular-hours description. Confirm which schedule applies before visiting, especially on Wednesday or Thursday, which the regular description marks closed.

Bring a pattern or project details when asking about supplies. The shop describes helping visitors choose yarn, coordinate colors and select tools. Its online catalog is useful for browsing, but confirm the quantity and color needed before assuming everything for a project is available in the shop.

For classes, use the current listing to verify the date, experience level, supplies and available places; a general classes link is not proof of an open seat. The contact page provides an enquiry form. Decide whether the immediate goal is shopping, social crafting or a structured lesson, then confirm the arrangement that matches it.
