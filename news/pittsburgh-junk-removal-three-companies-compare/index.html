Pittsburgh Junk Removal: Three Companies to Compare

Compare household junk removal by collection arrangements, service territory and quoting process, with Apex as our lead editorial selection for its detailed North Hills service guidance.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

A sofa in a basement and a pile of renovation debris can require different hauling arrangements. Pittsburgh-area residents comparing junk removal companies should start with the material, property access and service territory, then request a quote for the complete job.

Our lead editorial selection is Apex Junk Removal & Hauling because its website clearly explains truck-volume pricing, indoor collection and excluded materials. Two alternatives below offer different geographic coverage and enquiry options. We checked official websites October 13, 2026. This is a comparison of published service information, not an independent award, customer survey or report of personal experience.

1. Apex Junk Removal & Hauling: detailed North Hills guidance

Apex lists Northern Pittsburgh communities including Bellevue, Avalon, Shaler, Allison Park and Ross Township. Its residential menu includes furniture, appliances, basement and attic cleanouts, yard waste and electronics. It explicitly excludes hazardous waste, chemicals and tires.

The company says estimates are free and based on the space items occupy in its truck. It also says customers do not have to bring items to the curb: the crew collects from places including basements and backyards. That combination of collection guidance and exclusions explains our lead selection for readers planning a household clearout.

Call 724-680-6639 or use the website’s quote form. Describe stairs, narrow exits and the full inventory before agreeing to the work. Apex says it sorts for donation and recycling where possible; confirm the handling of particular items. Our Apex profile keeps the official contact and planning information together.

2. Junked Pittsburgh: compare southern service coverage

Junked Pittsburgh advertises service in Washington and Allegheny counties, naming Canonsburg, Washington, McMurray, Bridgeville and McDonald. It lists household and commercial removal, property cleanouts and light demolition of structures such as sheds and decks.

Its estimate process accepts calls, texts and online enquiries with photos. The published phone number is 412-905-9453. Pricing depends on the amount and type of material; the company says quotes include labor, hauling and disposal fees. Its large-item examples include appliances, hot tubs and safes.

This is an option to compare when the project combines bulky removal with a larger property cleanout. Ask whether dismantling is included and whether the proposed crew can handle the item’s weight and access route. Same-day or next-day service depends on availability. The company describes donation and recycling efforts, so ask what destination is appropriate for reusable pieces.

3. Pittsburgh Junk Company: photo enquiries and arrival windows

Pittsburgh Junk Company describes itself as a family-owned business based in the South Hills. Its service menu includes appliance and furniture removal, construction debris, estate cleanouts and commercial work. The website publishes 412-206-1125 for enquiries.

Customers can call, text a photo or use an online form to request an estimate. The company advertises two-hour arrival windows and an upfront price specific to the job. Those details make it worth comparing when coordinating access to a property or working around another appointment.

Ask whether a photo quote is provisional and what could change after the crew inspects the load. The company says it seeks to donate, repurpose and recycle as much as possible; that does not establish that every collected object will be reused.

Before booking any hauler, request written confirmation of accepted materials, the total charge, access arrangements and cancellation terms. Keep items intended for removal separate from belongings staying in the home. If reuse matters, discuss condition and destination before collection rather than assuming removal automatically means donation.
