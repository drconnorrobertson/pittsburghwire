Bob’s Discount Furniture Sets Cranberry Store Opening

The retailer’s new showroom is scheduled for Nov. 20 at 20111 Route 19, with a reported 28,400 square feet and about 20 local jobs planned.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 11, 2026 3 min read

Bob’s Discount Furniture is preparing to open a Cranberry Township showroom on Nov. 20 at 20111 Route 19, Suite B. The company’s Cranberry store page currently labels the location “Coming Soon” and lists the Route 19 address. The planned opening date, space and employment details were reported by the Cranberry Eagle on Oct. 8; the store is not yet open.

The 28,400-square-foot showroom is expected to provide approximately 20 jobs, according to the local report. It also says the first day is scheduled to include a ribbon cutting and $5,000 in check presentations to a local nonprofit and a school. The report does not identify the recipients, so shoppers should look for a later announcement from the retailer or township for those details.

A new option on Route 19

The address places the store in Cranberry’s established commercial corridor, where shoppers already use Route 19 for retail and services. Bob’s sells furniture and mattresses, and the company’s online store locator offers product browsing before a visit. The Cranberry page has not posted operating hours or an opening-day schedule yet, making its status as “Coming Soon” the clearest current guidance for people planning a trip.

For shoppers who need to browse sooner, Bob’s lists an operating North Hills location at 7377 McKnight Road in Pittsburgh. Its published hours are 10 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. Sunday. The store page says pickup is by appointment after an order is ready, so customers should check its current details before driving over.

The November target places the showroom ahead of the holiday shopping season, though no opening promotion has been announced. The Pittsburgh Business Times described it as Bob’s fifth Pittsburgh-area store and its second in the northern suburbs. Bob’s location pages also offer appointment booking and online shopping help; the Cranberry page will be the place to check whether those options have local hours or availability once the showroom opens.

What the announcement does—and doesn’t—confirm

The Cranberry store is a planned local opening from a national retailer, not a new Pittsburgh-area company. Bob’s official locator has established nearby Pennsylvania locations and is now preparing a separate listing for Cranberry. The reported job count and opening-day donations add a local community component, while the showroom itself gives northern Allegheny County another furniture-shopping option.

As of Oct. 11, the retailer’s page still shows the store as coming soon, with every day listed as closed and no phone number or store hours posted. The Nov. 20 date is the schedule reported by the Cranberry Eagle, and opening plans can change. Readers can check the official Cranberry location page for an updated status before visiting.
