Giulia Pittsburgh: Bloomfield Plans and Opening Status

Giulia is the Pittsburgh restaurant project from Lilith owners Jamilka Borges and Dianne DeStefano. Here is what their public filing confirms, and what remains unverified about opening and reservations.

By The Pittsburgh Wire June 19, 2026 2 min read

Updated October 10, 2026: Giulia is the Bloomfield restaurant project from Jamilka Borges and Dianne DeStefano, the operators behind Lilith. We have not verified a confirmed opening date, current Pittsburgh customer menu or Giulia reservation link from the official sources checked for this update.

Who is behind Giulia in Pittsburgh?

The operators' public Form C filing for Only Chives LLC, doing business as Giulia, identifies Borges and DeStefano as the owners. It describes a modern Italian restaurant with seasonal ingredients and plans for full-service dining, cocktails, catering and private events. Those are the project's stated plans, rather than a verified current customer offering.

Where is the Bloomfield project planned?

The same filing describes the former D'Amico's and Station space in Bloomfield and a goal of opening a second Pittsburgh restaurant in 2026. Its business plan discusses renovation work. A target year in a financing document does not establish that the restaurant is serving diners today.

Can I book Giulia or see its menu?

We could not verify a Pittsburgh Giulia booking page or current priced menu through the sources linked here. Before making a special trip, obtain confirmation of the opening, street address, service hours and reservation arrangements from the operators. A search result for another restaurant named Giulia should not be used as confirmation for this Pittsburgh project.

The operators' existing restaurant has its own official Lilith website, listing 238 Spahr Street in Pittsburgh and telephone 412-744-9290. That site identifies Borges and DeStefano and provides Lilith contact and reservation information. A Lilith booking is for Lilith; it should not be treated as a Giulia reservation.

What changed in this update?

An earlier version forecast a summer opening and presented preliminary dining details too definitively. We have replaced that forecast with the documented project status and removed unsupported quotations and equipment claims. This established page remains our reference for the Pittsburgh Giulia project; a confirmed opening announcement or customer booking page will provide the basis for the next update.
