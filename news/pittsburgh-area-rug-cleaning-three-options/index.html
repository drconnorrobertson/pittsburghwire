Pittsburgh Area Rug Cleaning: Three Options to Compare

Compare Pittsburgh-area rug cleaners by store drop-off, pickup arrangements, repair scope and published estimates, with World of Rugs as our lead editorial selection for its documented transport and restoration options.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

Pittsburgh area-rug cleaning involves a different handoff from an appointment to clean installed carpet. A removable rug may go to a cleaning facility, need edge or fringe repairs, and return by delivery. Comparing those arrangements first helps households decide whether to transport the rug themselves or arrange collection.

Our lead editorial selection is World of Rugs because its official information clearly offers both store drop-off and pickup, with separate cleaning and repair services. The alternatives below provide different booking and pricing information. This is an editorial comparison of published services checked October 13, 2026, not an independent award, customer-review ranking or report of personal testing.

1. World of Rugs: store drop-off or scheduled pickup

World of Rugs says customers can bring a rug to a store or schedule pickup and return after cleaning at one of its facilities. Its listed services include cleaning Oriental, wool, synthetic, handmade and machine-made rugs, plus repairs such as reweaving, overcasting, hand serging and re-fringing.

The Robinson Town Centre store is at 1955 Park Manor Boulevard, with published hours Monday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday, noon to 5 p.m. Call 412-787-7474. The Strip District location is at 1421 Penn Avenue, with posted hours Tuesday through Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday and Monday are closed. That store’s number is 412-471-6336.

Confirm the preferred drop-off location, transport charges and whether a repair requires a separate estimate. The company’s scheduling form says staff will follow up to confirm appointments and pickup details. Our sourced World of Rugs profile collects the two store contacts.

2. Model Cleaners: posted starting prices and facility cleaning

Model Cleaners’ rug service publishes starting prices by size and describes pickup and delivery as included. As checked October 13, an 8-by-10-foot rug starts at $80; unusual dimensions or shapes require a personalized quote. Confirm the final charge and address eligibility before arranging collection.

Its described process begins with inspection of fringe, edges, damage and dye stability. It then lists dust removal, hand washing, rinsing, water extraction and final grooming. The company says concerns are discussed before work starts, making intake questions particularly useful for a rug with an uncertain material label or an existing damaged edge.

Call 1-888-996-6335 or use its quote form. Ask what the proposed service includes, whether repairs cost extra and when the rug will be returned. Published starting prices are not a substitute for a confirmed estimate for your piece.

3. Hippo Carpet Cleaning: a pickup-based enquiry

Hippo Carpet Cleaning of Pittsburgh advertises complimentary rug pickup and delivery and lists 412-212-3700 for a telephone estimate. Its published contact hours are Monday through Sunday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; confirm collection availability directly.

The company describes assessment at its cleaning center, dust removal, cleaning suited to the material, spot treatment, drying and return delivery. Its page also mentions minor repairs. Ask how any repair is authorized and charged, and explain the rug’s construction, dimensions and problem areas when requesting the estimate. Confirm whether transport is included for your exact address.

For every provider, agree on who moves furniture, who rolls and carries the rug, the written scope and the return date. Discuss existing damage at intake. Clear collection and approval arrangements make these services easier to compare than a general promise to make a rug look new.
