Pittsburgh Tutoring Centers: Three Options to Compare

Compare Pittsburgh-area tutoring centers by subject scope, assessment process and scheduling, with Mathnasium of Squirrel Hill as our lead editorial selection for its clearly defined math program and enrollment steps.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

Pittsburgh-area tutoring centers offer different kinds of support, from a regular math program to help across several school subjects or coaching on study routines. Families can narrow the search by deciding whether the immediate need is subject knowledge, current assignments or the organization required to complete academic work.

Our lead editorial selection is Mathnasium of Squirrel Hill for its clearly defined math-only scope and published assessment-to-enrollment process. The other options broaden the comparison to several subjects and coaching. These selections use official service and access information checked October 13, 2026, rather than review scores, independent testing or promises about a student’s future grades.

1. Mathnasium of Squirrel Hill: a focused math program

Mathnasium of Squirrel Hill is at 6600 Forbes Avenue and lists PreK–12 math instruction, including arithmetic, algebra, geometry and trigonometry. Its enrollment process begins with an assessment, followed by a personalized learning plan and an agreed session schedule.

The center describes in-center instruction in a group environment and live online sessions. Its FAQ says the program follows a monthly membership model with an enrollment fee; contact the center for current amounts. Call 412-586-4534 or use its assessment-booking link to discuss the student’s goals.

Published instructional hours are Monday through Thursday, 2 p.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.; Friday and Saturday are closed. Confirm current openings and the recommended weekly commitment. Our sourced center profile gathers those details. Its focused subject scope is the reason for the lead selection; families needing several subjects should compare the alternatives.

2. Sylvan Learning of Pittsburgh: several subjects in McMurray

Sylvan Learning of Pittsburgh is located at 3150 Washington Road in McMurray, despite the Pittsburgh name. The local page lists K–12 tutoring in math, reading, writing and science, along with study skills, academic camps and test preparation.

Its published starting route is an assessment and discussion of learning goals. The page currently advertises a free assessment, but confirm the terms and subsequent program price directly. Use the local page’s booking or contact form so the enquiry is directed to the McMurray center.

Posted hours are Monday through Thursday, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., Friday by appointment, and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.; Sunday is closed. Ask which subjects can be included in one plan and whether sessions address school assignments, longer-term skills or both. Confirm tutor availability and the cancellation policy before committing.

3. Total Learning Centers: tutoring and school coaching

Total Learning Centers lists its address as 110 VIP Drive, Suite 101, Wexford. Its tutoring menu includes math, science, English, social studies and foreign languages for K–12 students, college learners and beyond. It also publishes online tutoring and coaching options.

The company separately describes school coaching around organization, time management and study techniques, offering a different enquiry when the concern extends beyond one subject. A free consultation is the published starting point; call 724-940-1090 or use its consultation form to discuss the appropriate service.

Posted hours are Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Friday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.; weekends are closed. Confirm the format, frequency, cost and location of the proposed sessions. Ask what progress information families receive and how the plan will be reviewed.

Bring specific learning goals and the available weekly schedule to each conversation. Compare the complete commitment, including assessment charges, enrollment terms and missed-session rules. A clearly defined plan is more useful than choosing by a general promise of academic success.
