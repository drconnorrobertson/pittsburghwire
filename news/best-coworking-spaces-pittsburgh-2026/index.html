Pittsburgh Coworking Spaces: 3 Options to Compare

Compare Alloy 26, Ascender and COhatch using official membership details, locations and day-pass options. Check access hours and meeting-room terms before booking.

By The Pittsburgh Wire May 5, 2026 6 min read

Updated October 10, 2026: Pittsburgh coworking options vary by access hours, meeting-room allowances and the type of community you want. This refreshed shortlist uses current official pages for three operators rather than repeating unverified addresses and amenities from our earlier ten-place list. These are editorial selections, not a scored ranking or personal review.

1. Alloy 26 — North Side workspace and drop-in parking

Alloy 26’s official site lists its Nova Place coworking address at 100 South Commons. A drop-in option is advertised at $30 per day with parking. Its published plans also include limited monthly visits, unlimited flex access and dedicated desks. Confirm the current terms and where to enter before arriving.

For a team that needs a door that closes, its website also lists short-term private office options at the Isabella location. Do not assume the private office and the Nova Place coworking floor are the same destination. Ask which address, hours and meeting facilities apply to your booking.

2. Ascender — East Liberty workspace and founder programs

Ascender’s membership page gives its address as 6401 Penn Avenue, Suite 300. Part-time flex membership is listed at $125 monthly and full-time flex at $225. The part-time plan specifies two days per week during regular business hours, while full-time access includes 24/7 entry.

The page distinguishes coworking from its Founder’s Club, dedicated desks and office suites. Compare the plan-level conference-room allowance rather than assuming every membership includes unlimited booking. Parking, printing and community programming are described on the official page, which also provides a tour enquiry.

3. COhatch — day passes and meeting access

COhatch’s day-pass page advertises a $35 coworking day pass and a $50 business day pass that includes one meeting-room hour. Both list phone-booth access, coffee, tea and Wi-Fi. Select the Pittsburgh location you intend to use and confirm availability before purchasing.

A day pass and a private office reservation are different products. Check whether your planned work needs a shared desk, a private call space or a room suitable for guests, and ask about any extra room charges.

Compare total cost and access, not just the desk price

Before booking, confirm arrival instructions, parking, guest rules, call-space availability and the hours your particular product allows. Ask whether meeting time must be reserved in advance and what happens when the included allowance is used. Rates listed here were checked on the linked official pages and may change.
