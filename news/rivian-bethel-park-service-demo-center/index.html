Rivian’s Bethel Park Center Adds Service and Demo Drives

Rivian’s operating Oxford Drive center combines vehicle service appointments with demonstration drives and a showroom, giving Pittsburgh-area owners and prospective drivers a South Hills contact point.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

Rivian’s Bethel Park Service + Demo Center gives Pittsburgh-area drivers a South Hills location for vehicle service, scheduled demonstration drives and a closer look at the company’s electric vehicles. The center at 1700 Oxford Drive is listed as operating on Rivian’s official location page. Its opening was also recognized in Senator Robinson’s September 26 community update, which congratulated the business on its new service and customer-experience facility.

The local page lists a vehicle showroom, Gear Shop, lounge and community events alongside demo drives and service appointments. It invites visitors to explore vehicle colors and materials and ask questions about electric-vehicle ownership. As checked October 13, general visitor hours are Tuesday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., with Sunday and Monday closed. Service hours are separately listed as Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., with weekends closed. The center’s published number is 412-896-7943. Confirm the relevant hours and appointment before travelling.

That distinction matters because looking around the vehicles and arranging repair work are different visits. Rivian’s local page says browsing does not require an appointment, while its service information says Service Center work is by appointment. Owners can report an issue and request service through the Rivian app or Service Support. The published process moves from a request to a repair plan, a scheduled time and approval in the app. A technician may visit the owner or the vehicle may need to be brought to a service location, depending on the work.

The Bethel Park location fits within a wider service-network expansion that Rivian described in a February 23 company update. At that time, Rivian said it planned more than 50 new Service Centers through 2027, taking the network beyond 150 locations by the end of that year. It also outlined a planned 50 percent increase in Mobile Service vans through 2026. Those are company-wide plans, rather than a statement about staffing or appointment capacity at the Oxford Drive facility. The local benefit is a listed physical service point within the South Hills.

Rivian’s broader service approach also includes remote diagnostics, software updates and mobile technicians. The company says connected diagnostics can identify issues early and, in some cases, allow a remote resolution. Its service guidance describes mobile work as appropriate for many tasks, while more extensive work may require a center visit. Owners should let the service team determine the suitable arrangement for the reported issue. A local center complements those options without establishing that every repair, part or appointment will be available immediately.

For prospective drivers, the practical starting point is the center’s demo-drive booking link; for existing owners, it is the app or service team. Confirm the vehicle available for a demonstration, the scheduled time and any visit instructions directly. For Pittsburgh’s developing electric-vehicle market, the Bethel Park facility adds a place where learning about a vehicle and planning its ongoing care can happen locally. That is a concrete addition to the region’s automotive-service infrastructure, with published information residents can use to plan a visit.
