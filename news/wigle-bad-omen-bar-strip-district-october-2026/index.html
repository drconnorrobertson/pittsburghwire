Wigle’s Bad Omen Bar Brings Folklore to the Strip

Wigle Whiskey’s October pop-up pairs six folklore-inspired cocktails with evening sessions through Halloween, a separate entrance and published hours to help plan a Strip District visit.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 10, 2026 3 min read

Wigle Whiskey is giving its Strip District distillery a seasonal folklore theme with Bad Omen Bar, an October Halloween pop-up at 2401 Smallman Street. The operator lists sessions through October 31, with no tickets required and entry through the side door facing the parking lot.

The official pop-up page identifies October 2 as opening night. Its concept draws on creatures from Pennsylvania and neighboring states, with Mothman serving as the bar's fictional host. For visitors making plans now, the remaining schedule offers several Friday and Saturday evenings plus a Thursday session during Halloween week.

When to visit in October

Saturday, October 10, is listed from 5–11 p.m. The following dates are October 16 and 23 from 6–11 p.m., and October 17 and 24 from 5–11 p.m. The final stretch adds Thursday, October 29, and Friday, October 30, from 6–11 p.m., followed by Halloween night, October 31, from 5–11 p.m.

Those are the dedicated Bad Omen Bar hours. They differ from the regular distillery schedule shown elsewhere on Wigle's website, so use the pop-up listing when choosing an evening. The no-ticket policy should also be read as an admission arrangement, rather than a promise of immediate seating. Call the distillery at 412-224-2827 with questions about a particular visit.

Six cocktails built around the theme

The published Bad Omen Bar menu lists six cocktails at $14 each, checked October 10. The Squonk combines Pennsylvania straight bourbon, spiced liqueur, vanilla and orange. Mothman pairs the namesake bourbon with Mountain Dew, house grenadine and cocktail cherries.

Albatwitch uses straight rye with apple liqueur, cider, lemon, graham syrup and a cinnamon-sugar rim. Giwoggle brings together spiced and white rum, lime, grenadine, coconut and aromatic bitters, served in a coconut cup. Bessie is a vodka drink with lemon, blue spirulina and sea-themed gummies, while Trotterkopf combines Dutch-style gin, honey, green tea, mint ginger ale and fresh mint.

The range gives a group several spirit bases to compare before ordering. Menu ingredients and prices can change, so review the current listing and ask staff about substitutions or dietary questions. These descriptions come from the operator's menu and do not represent tasting notes.

Pairing the pop-up with the distillery

Wigle's Strip District location page describes on-site spirits production, a cocktail bar and Saturday tours that include a tasting. Tour tickets are a separate offering from the unticketed pop-up. Someone planning both should check the tour reservation and the evening bar schedule independently.

The pop-up page says the Wigle Kitchen is available for dining before or after a visit. Its posted Friday and Saturday kitchen closing time is 9 p.m., earlier than Bad Omen Bar's 11 p.m. finish. Plan food around that earlier cutoff and confirm current kitchen availability rather than assuming the late bar hours extend meal service.

Wigle's company history connects its name to Pennsylvania distiller Phillip Wigle and the region's Whiskey Rebellion heritage. Bad Omen Bar adds a different kind of regional storytelling for a limited season. For the Strip District, it offers another reason to turn an October evening into a planned neighborhood visit, with the dates and menu available directly from the business.
