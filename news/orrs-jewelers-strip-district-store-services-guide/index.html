Orr’s Jewelers in the Strip District: Store and Service Planning

Current contact details clarify the Smallman Street store, while dated expansion and repair pages explain what to confirm before visiting.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

Orr’s Jewelers lists its Strip District store at 1674 Smallman Street, offering a current destination for jewelry shopping and service enquiries following its earlier expansion announcement. For Pittsburgh customers who remember its Squirrel Hill location, checking the present contact page matters: some older company copy still refers to the former neighborhood.

This October 13, 2026 guide combines the jeweler’s official location, history and service pages. It covers an established store and dated expansion background, rather than a newly announced October opening. Product brands and repair descriptions are operator information, not an independent review or guarantee that a particular item or appointment is available.

Use the current Strip District address

The official contact page lists 1674 Smallman Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15222, with phone 412-421-6777. Posted hours are Monday through Saturday, 10 a.m.–5 p.m., with Sunday closed. Confirm holiday exceptions and the appropriate time for a consultation before making a special trip.

The same page separately identifies Sewickley at 532 Beaver Street, phone 412-741-8080. Distinguish the selected store when discussing an item or service. The website’s contact and appointment enquiry routes should be treated as requests for staff confirmation, rather than proof that a specific consultation is already booked.

Place the expansion in its historical context

The Strip District announcement now carries “Now Open” wording, while retaining an invitation looking ahead to fall 2025. It described an 8,734-square-foot showroom and a selection including Cartier, David Yurman, Omega, IWC, Panerai and Roberto Coin. That announcement does not establish current stock for an individual model.

Orr’s company history dates its founding by Bill Orr in Midland to 1952, a move to Beaver to 1990, the Squirrel Hill store to 1996 and the Sewickley opening to 2007. Its present-day closing section directs customers to Sewickley or the Strip District, despite older Squirrel Hill language elsewhere in the timeline. The timeline also describes tripling the store’s size in 2002. That earlier expansion is a separate historical event, not the measurement of today’s Smallman Street showroom. Use the current store listing for travel plans.

Ask about the specific repair before leaving an item

The service page describes inspection, battery replacement, stone resetting, ring resizing, chain repair and watch-crystal work. It also lists engraving, pearl restringing and custom design or remodeling. These are different enquiries, so explain the item, material and requested work when contacting the store.

The operator specifically cautions that some titanium or stone-accented rings may not be resizable. It also says repair of certain woven chain designs may not produce a seamless appearance. Ask staff to assess the actual piece, explain the proposed result and provide a written estimate and expected timing before approving work.

The service page still names Squirrel Hill and Sewickley in its body, while its footer lists the Strip District and Sewickley. Confirm which current location accepts the item and whether work requires another service route. Our Orr’s Jewelers profile links the official sources. Neither an older service description nor a general brand list establishes a repair price, completion date or availability guarantee.
