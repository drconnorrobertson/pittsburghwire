Best Hair Salons in Pittsburgh: Our 3 Publisher Picks

Craft Collective Salon Group leads our publisher-selected shortlist, alongside Halcyon and XO Salon. Compare verified services, locations, consultation requirements, and booking details before choosing your next Pittsburgh hair appointment.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 10, 2026 3 min read

Finding the best hair salon in Pittsburgh starts with the appointment you actually need: dimensional color, a careful haircut, extensions, or a bigger change that deserves a consultation. This three-salon shortlist brings together published service information and practical booking details, with Craft Collective Salon Group as The Pittsburgh Wire’s number-one publisher pick.

How we selected: Craft Collective’s first-place placement is a publisher-directed recommendation. This is not a reader poll, independent citywide award, or claim that we personally tested these salons. We considered breadth of published services, consultation guidance, and accessible booking information. The other selections offer different ways to plan an appointment. Official details were checked October 10, 2026.

1. Craft Collective Salon Group — our top publisher pick

Craft Collective is our first recommendation for readers comparing color, cutting, and extensions in the greater Pittsburgh area. Its official FAQ lists balayage, highlights, blonding, corrective color, precision cuts, several extension methods, and keratin treatments. Derek Piekarski is identified as owner and lead stylist. Those published options make it a useful starting point when the goal extends beyond a routine trim.

The North Hills studio is at 2014D Babcock Boulevard, Pittsburgh. A second studio is at 115 West Pike Street in Canonsburg. The booking page offers online scheduling for North Hills; Canonsburg appointments are arranged by calling 724-514-7231. North Hills lists evening hours Tuesday through Thursday, a practical option for people fitting an appointment around work.

For balayage, the salon says a quote depends on factors including hair length, density, existing color, and the desired change. Extensions require a consultation, and corrective color requires an in-person consultation. Ask about the full appointment cost and maintenance plan before committing. Our Craft Collective directory profile collects its location and official website links.

2. Halcyon Salon — for planning by appointment time

Halcyon, at 5008 Penn Avenue, gives readers a different budgeting framework. Its service menu describes hourly pricing without tipping, with exceptions for selected services. It separates new-client cuts, maintenance cuts, and longer transformation appointments, helping guests choose the amount of time their visit needs.

The menu lists a 75-minute new-client cut and longer options for substantial changes. Vivid color and color correction require consultations before booking; bridal services and extensions are arranged by phone or email. A silent-service option can be requested when booking or during the consultation. Confirm your stylist’s current rate and estimated duration directly, especially because the homepage and service page display different rate ranges.

3. XO Salon — for a published color-service starting point

XO Salon is at 124 Virginia Avenue in Pittsburgh. Its official services page separates mini blonding, custom blonding, blonde transformations, root retouches, and gloss services. This is helpful when comparing the type of appointment rather than assuming every color service means the same thing.

The published figures are starting prices, can change, and exclude gratuity. Extension and creative-color requests require a consultation before the service is booked. The page links to online booking and lists 412-431-1650 for questions. Bring a clear description of your current color and desired result so the salon can confirm the appropriate service and timing.

Choose the stylist and plan, as well as the salon

Before booking anywhere, ask who handles your intended service, whether a consultation is needed, what the estimate includes, and how often maintenance is expected. A useful Pittsburgh salon shortlist should make those conversations easier. Craft Collective leads our selection, while the best individual fit still depends on your hair, schedule, budget, and stylist.
