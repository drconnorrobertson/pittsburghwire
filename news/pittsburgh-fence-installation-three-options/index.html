Pittsburgh Fence Installation: Three Companies and How to Compare

Compare material planning, repairs and combined fence-and-railing enquiries, with verified local contacts and practical questions for written estimates.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

A fence estimate is easier to compare when you know whether you need a new perimeter, repairs to an existing section or a gate and railing package. Pittsburgh-area contractors publish different ways to begin that conversation, from online layout tools to residential estimate forms and on-site consultations.

These three editorial selections were checked against official websites on October 13, 2026. We selected businesses with published residential services, local contact information and a clear enquiry route. Penn Fencing leads for its combination of material choices and an online planning tool. This is a practical shortlist, not an independent award, customer-experience review or guarantee of the lowest price.

1. Penn Fencing: Compare materials and sketch a layout

Penn Fencing’s residential page lists vinyl, aluminum, chain link, wood, composite, FenceTrac and aluminum privacy options. It links an instant estimator that lets visitors draw a layout and select materials and dimensions. That makes it a useful starting point for comparing configurations before discussing the actual property.

The business also sells materials, so distinguish a supply-only purchase from an installed project. An online estimate should start a conversation about the final scope; confirm measurements, site conditions and all included work before accepting a proposal.

Its headquarters and sales location is 647 Pittsburgh Road in Butler, with 724-586-7906 as the main phone. Published hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–3:30 p.m., with weekends closed. Confirm service at your address and whether a visit needs an appointment. Our Penn Fencing profile keeps the official service and enquiry links together.

2. Allegheny Fence: Ask about repair, removal or a new installation

Allegheny Fence publishes new installations, repairs, removal, welding, access control and material sales. Its residential product menu includes chain link, ornamental aluminum and iron, PVC, wood, gate operators and railings. The breadth is useful when an enquiry involves an existing fence rather than simply choosing a new panel style.

The contact page lists 4301 Irvine Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15207; 412-421-6005; and sales@alleghenyfence.com. Hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m., Saturday by appointment and Sunday closed. A residential estimate request is linked from that page.

For a repair, describe the material, damaged section and gate condition, and ask whether repair or replacement is being priced. Confirm whether removal and disposal of existing fencing are included in the written scope.

3. Pro Fence & Railing: Combine a fence enquiry with gates or handrails

Pro Fence & Railing lists new fence design and installation, replacement, wood and vinyl options, and gates and gate operators. Its official site also describes handrail services, making it worth considering when a project includes both yard boundaries and steps or a porch.

The company offers a free on-site design consultation for Pittsburgh, Bethel Park, Washington and nearby areas. Call 724-581-4209 or use the official enquiry form. Its company page identifies owner Jeffrey Urben and lists 2167 Mercer Road in New Brighton. Confirm address coverage and the appointment directly.

Compare the same scope before choosing

Request matching material, height, approximate length, gate dimensions and removal assumptions from each contractor. Ask about payment stages, scheduling and written warranty terms. For property within city limits, use the City of Pittsburgh zoning office to confirm project-specific requirements; suburban addresses should use their own municipality. A contractor’s estimate does not establish property boundaries or approval.
