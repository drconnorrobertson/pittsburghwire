Waterfront Apartments: Homestead Development Plans

County records describe a 266-unit Waterfront apartment proposal in Homestead and West Homestead. Read the documented plans and what remains unconfirmed.

By Megan StricklandFebruary 4, 20262 min read

Updated October 12, 2026: A proposed Waterfront apartment development spans Homestead and West Homestead. The Waterfront Apartments Tax Increment Financing Plan in Allegheny County’s legislative records, dated February 2024, describes 266 apartments above a 350-space parking garage. These are documented proposal figures, rather than confirmation of completed homes or current leasing availability.

Where is the proposed Waterfront apartment development?

The plan identifies approximately four acres at 185 West Waterfront Drive in Homestead and 201 West Waterfront Drive in West Homestead. It names Continental Real Estate Companies as the developer. Readers checking permits or project records should use these addresses and both borough names; a search for “Homestead apartments” alone can mix this proposal with existing rental properties.

What does the financing plan describe?

The document gives an approximately $90 million development budget and requests a 20-year tax increment financing incentive. It identifies garage construction and public infrastructure work, including trail relocation, utilities, road connections and stormwater management. A financing proposal explains the contemplated project and funding structure; it does not establish that every component has been built.

Are the apartments open or taking applications?

This update does not establish a current opening date, construction milestone or leasing launch. Before treating a projected completion date as a move-in opportunity, look for a current developer announcement or leasing page that identifies this specific property. An older plan, rendering or funding document should not substitute for a current availability statement.

Prospective residents can ask a leasing representative for the property’s official name, exact street address, available floor plans and earliest confirmed occupancy date. Request the complete monthly cost, including mandatory fees and parking, and distinguish a waitlist registration from an approved application or signed lease. If the project has not opened a leasing office, keep the inquiry separate from nearby existing apartment communities.

How to follow the project

Check the date and stage of each new record: proposal, financing decision, permit, construction update and occupancy are different milestones. Match the address and developer before combining reports. We will update this established article when a reliable new record resolves the current construction or leasing status.

Correction: Our original report stated 180 apartments, a Q3 2026 completion target, job totals, a pedestrian bridge and an attributed quotation without adequate supporting documentation. We have removed those claims and the unsupported historical details. The figures above are explicitly attributed to the dated public plan.
