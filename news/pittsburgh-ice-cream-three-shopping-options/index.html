Three Pittsburgh Ice Cream Options: Seasonal Scoops, Sandwiches and Gelato

Compare Millie’s, Leona’s and Mercurio’s by current location information, product format and the distinction between dairy-free and lactose-free labels.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

An ice-cream stop in Pittsburgh can mean a seasonal scoop, a wrapped sandwich to take home or gelato after a restaurant meal. Those formats have different availability and planning requirements, especially when a group needs to compare ingredients or collect dessert for later.

This October 13, 2026 shortlist uses official operator pages. Millie’s leads for its current fall flavor collection and location-by-location shopping information. Leona’s and Mercurio’s provide alternatives with different products and visit formats. These are editorial selections for practical usefulness, not independent awards or claims that we personally tasted and ranked every shop.

1. Millie’s: select a shop before choosing seasonal flavors

Millie’s 2026 Fall Favorites page includes pumpkin pie, caramel apple dumpling and butter pecan, alongside familiar flavors. It also separately labels options such as dairy-free mango and dairy-free fresh mint chip. The location selector helps shoppers narrow the list; the overall catalog is not a promise that every shop has every flavor.

The official location directory lists Market Square at 246 Forbes Avenue, 412-709-6579, and Lawrenceville at 3803 Butler Street, 412-621-1699. It marks North Park closed for the season, the CMU campus shop temporarily closed and factory tour bookings temporarily on hold. Check the selected shop before travelling.

For dessert at home, the homepage advertises a sundae box containing three pints, six toppings, six waffle cones and servingware. That is a separate purchase format from individual scoops. Our Millie’s profile links its flavor and location information.

2. Leona’s: distinguish real-dairy sandwiches from non-dairy desserts

Leona’s describes its ice cream as real dairy made lactose-free using an enzyme. That description should not be read as a non-dairy or vegan claim. The flavor catalog includes sandwich combinations such as blueberry on snickerdoodle and black raspberry ripple on sugar cookies, while warning that flavors vary by location and time.

The contact page identifies a scoop shop at 5119 Penn Avenue, but an older FAQ on the same page still says there is no scoop shop. Use the direct contact route, 412-709-5275 or info@leonaspgh.com, to confirm current scoop service and hours rather than treating that older answer as current.

For a larger gathering, the event page specifies a minimum of 60 sandwiches and two weeks’ lead time. Events are delivery or drop-off only; the company says it cannot attend and serve. Plan who will receive and distribute the order.

3. Mercurio’s Shadyside: plan gelato alongside a meal

Mercurio’s Shadyside combines gelato with a restaurant menu at 5523 Walnut Street, 412-621-6220. Published hours are noon–8 p.m. Sunday–Thursday and noon–9 p.m. Friday–Saturday. Seating closes approximately 30 minutes before closing; carryout ordering continues through closing.

The linked gelato menu still carries a March 2026 heading. It lists milk-and-egg gelatos separately from chocolate and lemon sorbettos labelled non-dairy and vegan. Treat those as published examples and confirm today’s selection before planning around a particular flavor.

For any shop, discuss ingredient and handling requirements directly and read the actual product label. Choose the format, location and collection time first, then confirm availability and the complete price for the order you want.
