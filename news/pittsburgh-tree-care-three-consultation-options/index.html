Three Pittsburgh Tree-Care Options: Consultations, Reports and Work Scope

Compare Northwood Tree Care, Davey and Bartlett by assessment services, local contact routes and the information needed for a useful estimate.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 13, 2026 3 min read

A Pittsburgh tree-care enquiry can involve pruning, plant health, a written assessment or removal. Those are different scopes of work. Before comparing prices, explain whether you want an opinion about a tree’s condition, a management plan or a crew to carry out a particular job.

This October 13, 2026 editorial shortlist compares three providers using official service and local-office pages. Northwood Tree Care leads for its explicit written-assessment service and practical access documents. Davey and Bartlett offer alternatives with established local contact routes. This is a comparison of published services, not an independent award, a review of completed work or a guarantee of an individual contractor’s performance.

1. Northwood Tree Care: distinguish an assessment from the work

Northwood’s service page lists pruning, plant health care, risk assessments and tree health evaluations. It says detailed written assessments are available as a service for property owners and tree managers. Ask whether a requested report is included in the consultation or separately charged, and specify who needs to use it.

The contact page lists call or text at 412-219-3040 and a mailing address at 8696 W Barkhurst Drive, Pittsburgh, PA 15237. It links an insurance certificate, neighbor-permission form and service terms. A mailing address should not be treated as a walk-in customer office.

Its service-area page covers Pittsburgh, North Hills and surrounding communities but says travel is limited. Confirm the property address. Our Northwood profile links the assessment and contact information.

2. Davey: use the local office for an on-site estimate

Davey’s Pittsburgh page lists pruning, removal and plant health care. It advertises free, no-obligation estimates and explains that tree size, condition, access and nearby structures make an in-person visit necessary for an accurate estimate.

Published office contacts include Gibsonia at 1023 Executive Drive, 724-444-8733; Monroeville at 200 Seco Road, 412-317-6142; and its South Pittsburgh office at 207 Freedom Drive in Lawrence, 724-746-8852. Confirm which office handles the property rather than choosing only by its marketing label.

A free estimate for proposed work should not automatically be treated as a detailed written report for another party. Describe the desired deliverable before booking, especially if you need documentation as well as a price.

3. Bartlett: identify routine service or written-plan enquiries

Bartlett’s Irwin office lists 119 Brush Creek Road, Irwin, PA 15642-9503, 724-863-8951 and irwinoffice@bartlett.com. Published office hours are Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–4 p.m.; those hours do not establish when a crew will arrive at your property.

The local page lists pruning, inspections, insect and disease management, and fertilization and soil care. Its appointment form distinguishes residential work, commercial work and environmental consulting for clients needing written plans. Soil-care information says samples are taken and nutrient status analyzed before fertilization is prescribed or performed.

For any provider, request a written scope identifying the trees, work, access arrangements, debris handling and any stump work. Compare insurance documentation and the proposed deliverables alongside price. Where neighboring-property access is needed, agree who will obtain permission and how the crew will reach the work area. Record what is included, what is optional and who will confirm the schedule before authorizing the job.
