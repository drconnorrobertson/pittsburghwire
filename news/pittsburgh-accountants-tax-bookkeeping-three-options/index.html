Pittsburgh Accountants: 3 Firms and Services to Compare

Compare Schneider Downs, Sisterson and Bookminders for tax, assurance and ongoing bookkeeping. Their official service descriptions show why choosing an accounting provider starts with the work you need.

By The Pittsburgh Wire October 10, 2026 3 min read

Choosing an accountant in Pittsburgh starts with defining the job: an annual tax return, an audit, or ongoing bookkeeping are different engagements. These three editorial selections cover distinct service models and publish useful information about their Pittsburgh presence. The order is for navigation, not an independent quality ranking; we have not tested their work or compared fees.

1. Schneider Downs — tax, assurance and managed accounting

Schneider Downs lists a Pittsburgh office at One PPG Place, Suite 1700, with telephone 412-261-3644. Its published services include audit and assurance, business and individual tax, and managed accounting. Within managed accounting, the firm distinguishes controller, CFO and full accounting services.

That breadth makes it an option to investigate when several financial functions need coordination. Start the enquiry with the actual deliverable you need: a return, recurring financial statements, or an assurance engagement. Ask which team would handle the work and whether the proposal includes ongoing support or a single project.

For an owner comparing outsourcing with hiring internally, request a clear division of responsibilities. Who supplies records, who approves payments, and who reviews the monthly reports? A service menu identifies capabilities; the engagement letter should explain what your particular business is buying.

2. Sisterson — Downtown assurance, tax and advisory work

Sisterson lists its office in the Union Trust Building at 501 Grant Street, Suite 450, Pittsburgh, PA 15219. The firm's telephone is 412-281-2025. Its service descriptions cover assurance, tax compliance and planning, and advisory work including valuations and transaction-related due diligence.

This is a useful starting point for readers whose accounting search involves a specific business event or reporting requirement. Describe that requirement before requesting a quote. If a lender or another party has requested a report, provide the wording of that request so the proposed engagement can be matched to it.

Ask about experience with your industry, the expected schedule and the records needed at the outset. Also establish your day-to-day contact. A firm's range of services does not establish that every engagement has the same scope, staffing or turnaround.

3. Bookminders — outsourced bookkeeping alongside your CPA

Bookminders' Pittsburgh location is listed at 101 South 10th Street, with telephone 412-323-2665. Its service overview describes ongoing outsourced accounting, including a weekly service model and a consultation to establish the client's needs.

The distinction for tax-season shoppers is explicit: its CPA partnership page says Bookminders does not perform tax, attest or consulting services. It instead works with CPA firms and prepares accounting records for their use. Do not treat a bookkeeping engagement as a substitute for a separate tax or assurance engagement.

When discussing a handoff, ask how your existing accountant will receive records, who resolves outstanding questions and how much historical cleanup is included. Explain which accounting software you use and whether your records are current before comparing proposals.

Compare scope before comparing the quoted fee

Send each prospective provider the same short brief: entity type, required work, deadline, current software and the condition of your records. Request written inclusions, exclusions, billing terms and communication arrangements. Confirm availability directly; this guide does not establish that any firm is accepting every type of new client.
