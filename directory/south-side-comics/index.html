South Side Comics

Comic Books and Subscription Service · Pleasant Hills, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

South Side Comics in the Pittsburgh area

South Side Comics appears in South Side Comics official information under Comic Books and Subscription Service. Its listed location is 314 1/2 Curry Hollow Road, Pittsburgh, PA 15236. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in Pleasant Hills and the Pittsburgh region.

Use the current Pleasant Hills address

South Side Comics publishes 314 1/2 Curry Hollow Road, Pittsburgh, PA 15236 as its store address. Its subscription page identifies the physical shop with Pleasant Hills. The business name should therefore not be used as a substitute for a current location: plan the trip using the published street address rather than assuming a storefront on Pittsburgh’s South Side.

The website separates new releases, back issues, graphic novels, manga and collectibles. Start with the product you want and whether you plan to collect it locally or have it shipped. Those are different fulfillment arrangements even when the same title is available through the online catalog.

Source: Use the current Pleasant Hills address — source information.

Subscriptions and preorders

The subscription portal describes Manage Comics for recurring series and special orders. It asks local customers to collect books regularly and offers invoicing to keep accounts active. For mail-order customers it describes weekly and monthly shipping choices. The page also says items ordered after the relevant final-order deadline must be paid for to continue using the service.

Before subscribing, clarify the series, format and any cover preferences. Ask how to change or end a request and how the store confirms that change. An account selection can create a purchase commitment under the shop’s terms, so it should not be treated as a casual wish list. Confirm any uncertainty with the store before the order deadline passes.

Source: Subscriptions and preorders — source information.

Choose collection or shipping deliberately

The portal asks customers to contact the shop if changing between in-store collection and shipping, or between weekly and monthly shipping. Keep the account details and preferred fulfillment method consistent rather than assuming that changing one setting updates every outstanding order automatically.

If you collect several series, consider how often you can reasonably visit and ask how that fits the store’s current pickup expectations. If you need shipping, check the charge and timing for the actual order. A general shipping banner does not establish that every item or every combination of products receives the same treatment.

Source: Choose collection or shipping deliberately — source information.

Orders containing future releases

The shipping policy says orders containing preorders ship when the last preorder item has been released. It separately describes an extended distribution window for exclusive-variant preorders and labels delivery dates as estimates. These details matter if an order combines an available book with one that is still awaiting release.

For a time-sensitive purchase, ask whether separate orders or another arrangement are appropriate. Distinguish the publisher’s release date, the shop’s dispatch timing and a carrier’s delivery estimate. They describe different stages. This profile does not convert any one of those dates into a guaranteed arrival at your door.

Source: Orders containing future releases — source information.

Returns, condition and contact

The published refund policy is dated March 22, 2023 and distinguishes categories it does not refund from conditional returns for graphic novels and novelty items. Read that currently posted policy before ordering and ask the shop about any unclear case. Its age is disclosed here rather than represented as a newly issued November policy.

The site publishes orders@southsidecomicspgh.com for enquiries. Include the order or item details when asking about a problem, and retain the seller’s description and correspondence. This entry was researched November 8, 2026 from official pages. It does not verify stock, independently grade comic condition or repeat the shop’s promotional service claims as a Pittsburgh Wire review.

Source: Returns, condition and contact — source information.

Separate a recurring order from a single purchase

Before starting a recurring order, decide how the comics will reach you and how often you expect to collect them. The portal describes different handling for local pickup and shipping, including weekly and monthly shipping choices. Those choices matter when comparing the timing of an ordinary order with an order containing a future release. Keep the series, issue and delivery preference together in an enquiry. A subscription request should not be interpreted as immediate stock confirmation, and the shop’s published shipping estimates should not be turned into a promised delivery date.

Source: Official operator information.
