3 Rivers Outdoor Company

Bicycle and Outdoor Services · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

3 Rivers Outdoor Company in the Pittsburgh area

3 Rivers Outdoor Company appears in 3 Rivers Outdoor Company official information under Bicycle and Outdoor Services. Its listed location is 1130 South Braddock Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15218. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in Pittsburgh and the Pittsburgh region.

A Regent Square outdoor shop

3 Rivers Outdoor Company, also called 3ROC, lists its main shop at 1130 South Braddock Avenue in Regent Square. Its retail categories span activities including camping, trail running, climbing, paddling and winter recreation. Those categories describe shopping scope, not a confirmation that every item or seasonal service is available today.

The current site separately lists an Aspinwall outpost as closed for the season and directs off-season visitors to Regent Square. This profile does not count the closed seasonal operation as another currently open storefront. Check the destination before arranging collection, especially when a saved listing points to River Avenue rather than South Braddock Avenue.

Source: Source: A Regent Square outdoor shop.

Background and community purpose

The business’s story page describes a mission around connecting outdoor participants, supporting use of regional outdoor places and encouraging reuse. It identifies Christine Iksic and explains that Jason Miller joined as a co-owner in 2022. Those are operator-provided historical details rather than a new ownership announcement made by this publication.

A community-oriented store and a booked outing have different roles. A reader can use the published mission to understand the business’s approach without treating it as confirmation that an event, guide or instructional place has been reserved. Ask about the specific activity and date if participation, rather than shopping, is the reason for the visit.

Source: Source: Background and community purpose.

Used gear is considered for consignment

The used-gear page describes staff review of clean, working outdoor equipment and seasonal acceptance. It distinguishes acceptable categories from exclusions, including used protective and climbing items it does not take. The published list should guide an enquiry, while staff retain the final acceptance decision. Do not assume every item with an outdoor brand can be left for sale.

Its payout presentation is not fully consistent: tiered percentages appear near the top, while a later process paragraph repeats a flat split. Confirm the written terms for the actual item before consigning. This profile deliberately does not select one percentage as universally applicable. The separate used-gear article explains how consignment differs from an immediate credit trade-in.

Source: Source: Used gear is considered for consignment.

Contact before transporting a collection

The contact page lists 412-871-5038 and gear@3riversoutdoor.com with the Regent Square address. It offers a route for purchase, order, return and warranty questions. Include the actual product or order details so staff can distinguish a retail enquiry from a consignment question or a request about a past event.

If bringing several pieces of used equipment, identify the quantity, condition and any missing components first. A contact-form message is not an acceptance decision or an agreed intake appointment. Keep the operator’s response with the item list and read the current agreement before transferring equipment. No valuation or tax advice is supplied by this profile.

Source: Source: Contact before transporting a collection.

Events and purchases need separate confirmation

The team page describes staff interests and experience across several outdoor activities. It is background about the people associated with the shop, not independent certification or a guarantee that a particular specialist is present at every opening. A technical product question should be directed to the business with the intended use clearly explained.

For a visit, settle whether the plan involves browsing new gear, buying a used item, proposing consignment or attending a scheduled activity. Each needs a different confirmation. Research was checked November 8, 2026; no store visit, equipment inspection, trip assessment or purchase was performed. The profile records the available operator routes without certifying the suitability of an item for an individual outdoor activity.

The consignment page also describes an unsold-item notice after 90 days and a limited collection period afterward. Read the current agreement for the actual choice about pickup or donation, and keep contact details current so the notice can reach you. Leaving an item for consideration should not be confused with agreeing to indefinite storage or a guaranteed sale.
