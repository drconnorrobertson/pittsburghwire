Game Masters

Tabletop Game Store · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

Game Masters in the Pittsburgh area

Game Masters appears in Official business service and visit information under Tabletop Game Store. Its listed location is 398 Perry Highway, Pittsburgh, PA 15229. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in Pittsburgh and the Pittsburgh region.

A game shop on Perry Highway

Game Masters lists its store at 398 Perry Highway, Pittsburgh, PA 15229, with telephone 412-367-4414. The website distinguishes retail browsing, the Dragon’s Roast café, table requests and organized events. Decide which part of the business you need before treating one schedule or fee as an answer for the entire visit.

The store calendar includes board-game, role-playing and trading-card formats. A dated event is a better planning source than a general category description, particularly when deciding whether a session is suitable for a newcomer. Ask about the chosen event’s requirements and availability instead of assuming every scheduled game is open to additional players.

Source: A game shop on Perry Highway — source information.

Table play and the game library

The Run Your Game page lists table-space rental at $5 per person, including access to the game library. It separately describes take-home board-game rentals, with a percentage-based weekly charge and a minimum. These are different services: paying to play at a table does not automatically arrange taking the game home.

For a rental, ask about the particular title, return date, completeness and current charge. If you hope to buy a new copy afterward, confirm the shop’s stated rebate conditions and whether that title can still be purchased new. The published policy notes that some library games are out of print, so a general rental offer is not a promise of a later retail copy.

Source: Table play and the game library — source information.

Submitting a table request

The linked table-request route uses the store’s event-submission system. The operator’s guidance asks for information such as the game, date, time, organizer and whether the group is open, looking for players or closed. Requests require review before appearing on the calendar; submitting one is not approval of a table.

If you are inviting other people, wait for the shop’s confirmation and clarify any charge beyond ordinary table use. Describe the expected group size and how long you intend to play. Keep changes with the original request so the organizer and store are working from the same plan. Do not rely on a tentative calendar entry as a confirmed private booking.

Source: Submitting a table request — source information.

The café is a separate schedule

Dragon’s Roast café has its own published operating hours, including earlier opening than the game store. Use the relevant schedule when planning breakfast, a coffee stop or an evening game. A café opening time does not establish that every retail or gaming service is available at that hour.

If food is part of an organized session, ask how the group should order and what arrangements apply. Keep any access or participation needs specific when enquiring. The site describes amenities, but a general feature list should not replace a discussion of the particular assistance or space your group requires.

Source: The café is a separate schedule — source information.

Parking notices and current information

An October 2, 2026 notice describes temporary parking arrangements during work at the front of the building. It directs visitors to alternate areas. The continued presence of the notice does not independently establish the construction’s current stage, so contact the store before a visit if the route or parking arrangement matters.

The shop’s instructions also distinguish a short retail visit from a longer playing session when discussing parking. Check current signs and the operator’s advice rather than assume any nearby space is designated for the shop. This profile was researched November 8, 2026 from official pages and does not claim a personal visit or guarantee a table, a rental or a place in a game.

Source: Parking notices and current information — source information.

Bring enough information for an event request

For an organized game, include the game system, intended date and time, expected participation and whether newcomers may join. The published request process distinguishes an open group from a full or closed table and asks for organizer contact details. That gives the shop information to review, rather than creating an automatic booking. If a meeting also depends on cafe service, confirm that schedule separately. The cafe and retail operation publish different hours, and a cafe opening does not establish that the game store or a private table is open.

Source: Official operator information.
