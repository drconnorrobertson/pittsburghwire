Pittsburgh Comics

Comic Books, Graphic Novels and Subscriptions · McMurray, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

Pittsburgh Comics in the Pittsburgh area

Pittsburgh Comics appears in Pittsburgh Comics official information under Comic Books, Graphic Novels and Subscriptions. Its listed location is 113 E. McMurray Road, McMurray, PA 15317. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in McMurray and the Pittsburgh region.

A South Hills shop with a regional name

Pittsburgh Comics lists its store at 113 East McMurray Road, McMurray, PA 15317, with telephone 724-941-5445. Despite the regional business name, the shop is outside Pittsburgh proper. Use the full street address when planning a visit rather than searching only for a city-center location.

The store-information page publishes opening hours, including a different Wednesday schedule. Check the current page and any dated shop notice before traveling. A scheduled new-release day can explain a special opening window without establishing that a particular issue is already on the shelf or held for you.

Source: A South Hills shop with a regional name — source information.

New issues, back issues and collected editions

The shop page describes new and back-issue comics, trades, graphic novels, games and collectibles. It also links online shopping and a subscription route. Those categories support different enquiries: finding a current issue, filling a gap in a collection or choosing a collected story to read without following individual releases.

For a specific request, give the title, issue or volume number and the edition or cover you want. If you are unsure, describe whether you want a reading copy, a collected storyline or a particular collectible version. A broad title can appear in several formats, so identifying the intended product is more useful than assuming every listing with that name is equivalent.

Source: New issues, back issues and collected editions — source information.

Pull lists are ongoing arrangements

The official shopping page invites customers to set up pull lists or subscriptions for books held at the counter. It links to ComicHub for that process. Before starting, ask the store how ordering, payment, pickup and changes are handled. A subscription enquiry is not simply a reservation for one already-published issue.

Keep the series and any cover preferences clear, and ask which requests need advance notice. If you stop collecting a title or need to change the pickup arrangement, contact the store rather than assuming that not collecting a book cancels the request. The shop’s current agreement should control those details; this profile does not invent account terms that are not published.

Source: Pull lists are ongoing arrangements — source information.

Business background and community publishing

The about page explains the store’s earlier relationship with New Dimension Comics and its later independent Pittsburgh Comics identity. It describes goals of a welcoming shop and space for local creators. The page uses relative historical wording, so this profile does not calculate a founding year from an undated statement about how many years have passed.

The website’s podcast page identifies Colin McMahon as the owner and introduces the shop’s comics discussion program. Treat the podcast and archived posts as the store’s own commentary, rather than independent reviews of its retail service. A historical episode or announcement can provide background without establishing a current event date.

Source: Business background and community publishing — source information.

Read dated arrival information carefully

The homepage publishes dated incoming-comic lists and shop announcements. At review it linked a list for Wednesday, November 11, 2026. An arriving list identifies a planned release or delivery context; it does not confirm that a specific copy has arrived, remains available or has been assigned to an individual customer.

Use the current shop page or contact form for an actual purchase question. Include the relevant listing or issue information so the response can address the right item. This profile was checked November 8, 2026 from official sources. It does not claim a firsthand shop visit, grade a collectible or promise future resale value.

Source: Read dated arrival information carefully — source information.

Prepare a specific shop enquiry

When asking about a comic, include the publisher, series title, issue number and desired edition. A single issue, a collected paperback and a special cover can refer to different purchases even when the story has the same name. The shop’s published product categories support that distinction, but they do not establish that each edition is on the shelf. If the trip depends on one item, ask the shop to confirm the edition and collection arrangement before traveling. A forthcoming release post is a dated plan rather than proof that a copy has arrived or been reserved.

Source: Official operator information.
