Cut & Sew Studio

Sewing Classes and Studio Access · Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

Cut & Sew Studio in the Pittsburgh area

Cut & Sew Studio appears in Cut & Sew Studio official information under Sewing Classes and Studio Access. Its listed location is 1747 Chislett Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15206. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in Pittsburgh and the Pittsburgh region.

A sewing studio in Morningside

Cut & Sew Studio teaches sewing at 1747 Chislett Street in Morningside. Its official history identifies Catherine Batcho as the owner and dates the business to December 2007. The studio describes an earlier East Liberty space, a move to Bryant Street in Highland Park and its later Chislett Street location. Those are published business-history details, not a report of a new November opening.

In April 2026, Firecracker Fabrics moved across the street to 1801 Chislett Street, and Cut & Sew expanded into the previously shared space. The two businesses remain related resources for students, but they have separate addresses and roles. Use the studio address for class attendance and the fabric shop address for ordinary supply pickup.

The studio’s owner biography describes Batcho’s fashion-design education and work in design, product development and technical design before returning to Pittsburgh. That background helps explain the range of pattern and construction topics presented on the site. It remains a published biography, rather than evidence that every instructor teaches every technique or that a specific course is currently scheduled.

Source: A sewing studio in Morningside — official source.

Choose instruction by the skills you already have

The studio’s class-level guide distinguishes students who have never used a machine from those comfortable threading it, winding a bobbin and adjusting stitches. Later levels build toward new fabrics, construction methods, pattern manipulation and fit. These descriptions help match a course to experience; they do not assess an individual student without a conversation.

If you are unsure about a prerequisite, explain the tasks you can perform independently and the parts that still require help. Someone who can stitch a straight seam may need a different next course from someone who wants to alter a pattern. Choose a listed class because its curriculum matches that next step, rather than because its title sounds broadly beginner-friendly.

Source: Choose instruction by the skills you already have — official source.

Open studio and assisted work are different formats

Open studio rents access to sewing equipment and workspace for people with suitable experience. The page distinguishes it from an assisted open workshop, where a teacher answers project questions. It also describes sewing machines, cutting tables, irons and other equipment, with conditions on serger use and heavy projects. Supplies are not included.

The dated session visible at review was November 3, already past. Contact the studio about a future opportunity instead of treating that listing as an upcoming date. If you need help with construction or fitting, ask about instruction rather than relying on a workspace booking to include project assistance.

Source: Open studio and assisted work are different formats — official source.

Plan supplies before arriving

The FAQ explains the separate Firecracker pickup arrangement and asks students to prepare ahead. Class descriptions and messages from the studio identify the required materials. The April 2026 update takes precedence over older wording suggesting supplies are all in the same building. Check the shop’s hours and wait for order readiness before planning a collection.

Use the supply list for the selected project, including the correct fabric type, notions and any pattern. Ask the studio about substitutions before buying them. If a class kit has a special delivery arrangement, confirm that arrangement explicitly; it should not be generalized to every online order. Students should also check the specific machine instructions before bringing their own equipment.

Source: Plan supplies before arriving — official source.

Registration and visiting

The registration policy distinguishes cancellation windows for different class formats and generally provides credit rather than an automatic cash refund. Longer courses and children’s programs have separate notice requirements. Read the current policy alongside the actual course listing before paying, especially when the program spans several dates.

The studio’s about page says it does not have general drop-in visiting hours. People who are not registered should arrange a convenient visit by email. The FAQ provides 412-865-6565 for studio enquiries. This profile was researched November 8, 2026 from official material; it does not claim firsthand instruction, available seats or an independently measured teaching outcome.
