Hollowood Music and Sound

Music Retail and Services · McKees Rocks, Pennsylvania

By Dr. Connor Robertson · Updated November 8, 2026

Hollowood Music and Sound in the Pittsburgh area

Hollowood Music and Sound appears in Hollowood Music and Sound official information under Music Retail and Services. Its listed location is 601 Chartiers Avenue, McKees Rocks, PA 15136. This profile brings the location, website, and available contact information together for readers exploring businesses in McKees Rocks and the Pittsburgh region.

Several music services at one address

Hollowood Music and Sound lists 601 Chartiers Avenue in McKees Rocks and 412-771-3060. Its website separates new and used retail equipment, repairs, guitar lessons, commercial installations and pro sound. The immediate planning question is which of those services a visitor needs. Buying an amplifier and arranging sound for an event are different conversations even when the same business can discuss both.

The homepage also points to Hawthorne Drum Shop on the second floor. That is a separately identified drum business with its own website and sales terms, not a reason to assume one retailer’s policy governs every transaction in the building. Use the business name and floor when arranging a collection or asking about a listed drum item. This profile does not assess step-free access or make a claim about the building’s layout beyond the operators’ location statements.

Source: Source: Several music services at one address.

New, used and consignment enquiries

The operator invites people with guitars, amplifiers and PA equipment to ask about selling or consigning used gear. A sale and a consignment are distinct arrangements: one concerns transferring the item directly, while the other requires agreement about how it will be offered. The website’s general invitation is not an accepted offer for every item brought to the shop.

Prepare a description of the model, condition, modifications and included accessories. Ask how the chosen arrangement works before leaving equipment behind, including who sets the price and how collection or payment is handled. No commission rate or payout date is invented here. The local trade guide linked below compares other operators’ enquiry routes without supplying terms that Hollowood has not published.

Source: Source: New, used and consignment enquiries.

Repair scope and estimates

Hollowood’s repair page lists guitar and bass setups, fretted-instrument work and amplifier or PA repairs. It publishes starting charges and a diagnostic structure for electronics, so a headline setup figure should not be treated as the total price for an unrelated problem. Describe the fault and the equipment before asking for an estimate.

For an instrument, distinguish routine setup preferences from a broken component or structural issue. For audio equipment, identify what stops working and whether the problem depends on another part of the system. Ask which accessories the technician needs to assess the problem. This is preparation for a service conversation, not troubleshooting advice or a promise that repair is possible. Confirm the approval process for additional work and collection arrangements with the shop.

Source: Source: Repair scope and estimates.

Pro sound is more than a product rental

The pro-sound page describes production services for different scales of events, from smaller gatherings to larger performances. That is a separate service description from the retail catalog. An organizer should identify the event type, venue, schedule and intended sound requirements before asking which support can be provided. A published range of event experience does not establish availability on a selected date.

Ask whether the proposal includes operators, transport, setup, soundcheck and the equipment needed for the actual performers or speakers. Do not infer those elements from an equipment name alone. If another supplier is furnishing part of the system, make that clear. The event-audio guide below compares a production enquiry with a customer-collected rental so the organizer can ask for the right arrangement.

Source: Source: Pro sound is more than a product rental.

Teaching has its own registration conversation

The lesson page describes guitar instruction and contains a scholarship account with relative historical wording. It does not establish a currently open scholarship place or a future deadline. Ask about today’s teacher availability and registration rather than presenting the retained account as a newly announced program.

This profile was checked November 8, 2026 from the operator’s pages. It does not report a lesson, repair, purchase or event booking. Keep the desired service and exact location together when contacting the business; a general retail enquiry should not be mistaken for confirmation that a technician, instructor or production crew has been reserved.
