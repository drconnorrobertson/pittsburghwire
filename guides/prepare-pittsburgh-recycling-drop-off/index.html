Prepare a City of Pittsburgh Recycling Drop-Off

Preparing a recycling drop-off in Pittsburgh begins with the exact objects, not just the material words on a box. A plastic tub and a plastic clamshell can belong to different listed categories. A glass bottle and a drinking glass are not interchangeable items. A shipment can contain cardboard and several kinds of packing material. Sorting those distinctions before choosing a destination makes the question more precise and helps avoid treating a general recycling label as permission to place every component in one container.

This guide uses the City of Pittsburgh's official recycling and drop-off pages as read October 27, 2026. It concerns the City's stated streams, not every private recycler or municipality with a Pittsburgh mailing address. Requirements, accepted items and site arrangements can change.
