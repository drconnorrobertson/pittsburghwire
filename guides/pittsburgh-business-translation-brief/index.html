Pittsburgh Business Translation Quotes: Audience, Review and Format

Compare translation proposals by language variant, purpose, terminology, review level and final deliverables.

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

A translation request needs more than a language pair and word count. The audience, purpose, technical vocabulary and final format affect the assignment. Prepare the same brief for Pittsburgh-area language providers so a low price does not hide a different review process or missing production work. This guide does not verify credentials or establish whether a translation meets an agency’s requirements.

Identify the actual audience and use

Record the source language, target language or regional variant, audience and purpose. Separate internal reference material from customer-facing or official submissions. If a recipient has specific requirements, obtain them directly and share them with candidates. Do not assume one format or credential is accepted by every organization.

Inventory the source material

Provide the document types, approximate volume, file formats and any images or scanned pages requiring additional work. State whether the source is final. Mark sections that should remain unchanged. A later revision to the original document can create translation, review and layout work that was absent from the first estimate.

Clarify terminology and review

Supply approved product names, a glossary and relevant reference material. Ask who translates, who reviews and how questions are resolved. Describe any specialist knowledge needed without assuming that a general language-service description proves it. For a fictional technical brochure, a terminology review may be as important as the initial translation.

Specify formatting and confidentiality

Ask whether the quote includes layout, editable files, proofreading after layout and other production tasks. Agree on how confidential documents will be transferred and accessed. NIST’s small-business material offers background for discussing responsibility with external providers; it does not certify a particular provider’s controls.

Compare delivery and revision terms

Confirm due dates, staged delivery, revision rounds and the treatment of source changes. Request the actual fee basis and minimum charges instead of assuming every quote uses the same word count. Name the internal reviewer and final approver. Keep the source version, glossary, questions and approved output together for future updates.

Prepare this before asking for quotes

  • Audience, purpose and language variant
  • Final source inventory and formats
  • Terminology and review process
  • Secure transfer and layout requirements
  • Delivery stages and revision terms
