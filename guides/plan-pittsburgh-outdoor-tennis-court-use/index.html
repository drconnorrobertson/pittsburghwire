Plan Pittsburgh Outdoor Tennis Around the Court, Period and Proposed Use

The distinction matters whether you want a casual hit, a recurring practice, a clinic or an organized event. Identify the exact manager, court, activity and period before treating a map marker, calendar entry or saved screenshot as evidence of permission to use the space.
