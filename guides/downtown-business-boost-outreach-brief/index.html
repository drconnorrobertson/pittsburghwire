Downtown Business Boost: Prepare an Outreach and Promotion Brief

Use the PDP Business Boost contact route with a clear customer question, accurate business details and a practical promotion brief.

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 21, 2026

A Downtown merchant can prepare for a useful promotional conversation before buying another campaign. Business Boost offers a named route into the Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership’s business outreach. The useful starting point is a small, testable question about reaching customers, supported by information your team can keep current. A listing, meeting or promotional mention should never be treated as evidence that a particular number of sales will follow.

Read the phase before planning around it

Checked November 21, 2026, the PDP page describes phase-one outreach and promotion, with later marketing-agency support planned for a second phase. It offers a Business Boost mailing-list route. That wording does not establish that agency coaching is currently available to your business. Ask what is active, how participation works and which materials are useful before placing an outside agency assignment on hold.

Choose one customer question

Write a question your operation can actually answer: are nearby office workers aware of the weekday pickup window, or are weekend visitors finding the entrance? These are different assignments. The first may need a timely menu and pickup instructions; the second may need a clear exterior photo and arrival information. Provide your own observations and their dates. Keep a staff impression separate from counted enquiries, transactions or website visits.

Prepare an accurate promotion packet

Use one approved business name, public address, website, current opening hours and a contact who can approve changes. Supply a short description in ordinary language, one image you have permission to share and a link that works on a phone. Put temporary offers and end dates in a separate field. Explain any booking requirement, limited stock or access detail in the copy so a promotional mention does not create an expectation your staff cannot meet.

Worked example: a weekday pickup test

Imagine a fictional lunch shop with a staffed pickup window from noon until two. Its brief asks whether the PDP has a relevant channel for a two-week awareness test. The shop supplies an approved menu link, the exact dates and a named responder. It records pickup enquiries separately from completed orders. A busier week could reflect weather or another event; the owner therefore treats the result as a lead for further testing, not a calculated return attributable to PDP.

Close the loop without assuming placement

After an introductory conversation, record what was requested, who owns the next step and when information expires. If the answer is newsletter-only participation today, maintain the packet for that channel. If someone requests revisions, retain the approved version and send corrections promptly when hours change. Before commissioning paid creative work, clarify whether it is needed for the available opportunity and who owns the resulting files.

Your next-step working file

  • A single customer question and the observation behind it.
  • Approved public details, working mobile link and image-use permission.
  • Start/end dates for anything temporary.
  • Questions about the currently active phase and participation process.
  • One owner for approvals, corrections and a dated follow-up.
