Pittsburgh Office Furniture Installation: Site and Scope Checklist

Compare furniture installation proposals using inventory, layouts, access, assembly and closeout responsibilities.

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Office furniture installation involves more than putting desks together. Delivery access, missing components, room layouts and disposal can affect both the schedule and the quote. Prepare a site-specific brief for Pittsburgh-area installers before requesting prices. This is an assignment checklist, not a certification of a provider or advice about structural, electrical or workplace requirements.

Create the installation inventory

List products, quantities, model references and whether items are new, used or being relocated. Attach assembly information available from the manufacturer. Identify parts supplied by the business and parts the installer must obtain. A count of workstations does not explain storage, screens, power accessories or other components.

Confirm the layout and site readiness

Provide the approved layout, room dimensions and any access constraints. State which areas will be occupied during the work and who can authorize a placement change. Ask the installer to identify prerequisites and work outside its scope. Do not assume furniture assembly includes electrical connections, wall alterations or other specialist work.

Describe the delivery route

Record loading access, elevator arrangements, permitted working hours and building contacts. Confirm the rules with the property manager rather than assuming every downtown or suburban building uses the same process. Identify storage space for cartons and protection needed for existing finishes. These details should be consistent across bidders.

Separate assembly from extra services

Ask whether the proposal includes unloading, unpacking, assembly, placement, adjustments, packaging removal and removal of old furniture. For a fictional office adding ten desks, distinguish the ten installations from moving existing equipment or disposing of old units. Clarify how missing parts, damaged deliveries and return visits are charged.

Agree on completion evidence

Use an item checklist and walkthrough to record completed work and unresolved issues. Name the business contact who can sign off, while preserving any remaining punch-list items. Keep manufacturer information and installer notes accessible. Directory presence does not verify insurance, availability or suitability; confirm those matters directly for the actual assignment.

Prepare this before asking for quotes

  • Product inventory and manufacturer references
  • Approved layout and site prerequisites
  • Loading, elevator and working-hour details
  • Assembly, disposal and return-visit boundaries
  • Walkthrough and unresolved-item process
