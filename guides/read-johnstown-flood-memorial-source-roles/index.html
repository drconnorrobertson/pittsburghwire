Read Johnstown Flood National Memorial Through Landscape, Testimony and Source Roles

Johnstown Flood National Memorial offers several ways to approach the same historical event: a surviving landscape, visitor-center interpretation, a survivor account, photographs, a film and commemorative attention to those who died. Each source has a different role. Begin by asking what you want to understand and which material can answer that question. A view of a dam remnant, a later explanatory map and a reproduced record should not become one undifferentiated body of original evidence.

This guide uses National Park Service pages read October 29, 2026, and six original fictional practice cases. It reports no actual visit, film playback, image inspection, original-record review, structural examination or route survey. The invented identifiers, quantities and 2030 dates are teaching inputs. Their checks establish limited role, locator, date and allocation relationships.
