About The Founder

Dr. Connor Robertson

Dr. Connor Robertson is a Pittsburgh-based entrepreneur, author, and business strategist. He founded The Pittsburgh Wire to spotlight the people, businesses, and developments shaping the city's future.

With a background spanning business strategy, real estate development, and entrepreneurship, Dr. Robertson brings a unique perspective to Pittsburgh's business landscape. Robertson is also the author of six books on business strategy and wealth building, the host of The Prospecting Show (178+ episodes), and the founder of Elixir Consulting Group, The Grant Finder, and Seymour Maison. His passion for the city's potential drives The Pittsburgh Wire's mission to celebrate the builders, founders, and visionaries transforming Pittsburgh.

Dr. Robertson is committed to telling Pittsburgh's forward-looking stories: the entrepreneurs launching ambitious ventures, the developers betting on neighborhoods, the real estate investors who see what's next, and the professionals choosing to build their careers in this remarkable city. He believes Pittsburgh's greatest competitive advantage is its authenticity, its people, and its relentless optimism.

Beyond The Pittsburgh Wire, Dr. Robertson is an active voice in Pittsburgh's business community, sharing insights on entrepreneurship, strategy, and the city's evolving economy. Learn more at drconnorrobertson.com.
